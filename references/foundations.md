# Foundations and limits

## Durable design as a compilation target

The abstract of [Design Docs Are All You Need: An AI-native Machine-Learning Performance Tool](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.05364) describes SMART as a system whose design documents form a dependency graph and whose implementation is regenerated by coding agents. The abstract was verified during the v2 revision; full HTML retrieval remained unavailable. Limit attribution to that documented framing rather than unverified implementation details.

This skill adopts durable specifications and environment-specific regeneration as design choices. Neither that framing nor the paper title establishes deterministic compilation, equivalent generated programs, scientific validity, or the recovery protocol below. Those properties need independent checks.

## Discovery history and exploration policy

Dream-RSI: Recursive Self-Improvement through Evolving Worlds, https://arxiv.org/html/2609.14858v1, describes programmable exploration while leaving the coding agent fixed. Recorded discovery trees include saved workspaces and realized outcomes; alternative exploration policies can traverse recorded branches without rerunning those experiments. Policy refinement operates on the exploration layer rather than model-weight updates.

Borrow its preservation of parent relationships, artifacts, observations and costs. A research archive plus a summary is only groundwork for replay. It is not itself the paper's replay simulator or recursive policy optimization. Actual replay requires a shared action interface, restricted revealed outcomes, historical cost accounting and policy evaluation over observed branches. Validate improved policies prospectively; unobserved branches are not scored by imagination.

## Portability

Preserve schemas, reducer semantics and artifact contracts across implementations. Regeneration must pass the bundled protocol fixtures against its own reducer and separately exercise filesystem recovery and real evaluator controls. The reference reducer defines the shared replay projection; it does not prove domain science or platform durability. An implementation language change does not justify erasing state or changing metric meanings. Version incompatible contracts and provide explicit migration behavior.
